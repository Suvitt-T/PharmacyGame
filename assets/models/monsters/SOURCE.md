# Monster models — third-party assets

Source: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com) — "Ultimate Animated Monsters" pack.

License: Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0 — https://quaternius.com/license.html

Summary of the terms that matter here: commercial use is allowed and no attribution
is required, but the assets may not be resold or redistributed as a standalone asset
pack. They are included here as part of this game, not as a pack for redistribution.

Each `.gltf` is self-contained (geometry, skin, texture and 14 animations are all
embedded), so no companion `.bin` or texture files are needed.

Shared animation names across every model: `Death`, `Duck`, `HitReact`, `Idle`,
`Jump`, `Jump_Idle`, `Jump_Land`, `No`, `Punch`, `Run`, `Walk`, `Wave`, `Weapon`, `Yes`.
`src/actors/actor_visuals.gd` maps game states onto these names.
